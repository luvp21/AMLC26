# Amazon ML Challenge 2026 — official rules & timeline

Source: contest email from the Amazon ML Challenge 2026 organizing team (Unstop).
Kept here verbatim-in-substance so the rules survive independent of that email.

## Timeline

| Date (IST) | Event |
|---|---|
| 7–22 Sep 2026 | Registration |
| 21 Sep 2026, 5–6 PM | Prep virtual session (recording on Twitch — see `infra/aws/README.md` for the free-credits/SageMaker notes it covered) |
| **25 Sep 2026, 12:00 AM – 27 Sep 2026, 11:59 PM** | **The 72-hour challenge window** |
| 2 Oct 2026 | Top 100 teams announced |
| 7 Oct 2026 | Grand finale — top 10 present to Amazon Scientists |

Problem statement and dataset drop on day 1 (25 Sep); submissions close end of day 3 (27 Sep).

## Eligibility

- Engineering students in India: B.E./B.Tech/M.Tech/M.S./PhD, graduating 2027 or 2028.
- Team size 2–4, cross-college teams allowed.
- Valid AWS Builder Center profile ID required.
- Desktop/laptop only — no mobile devices.
- **One login per participant, no simultaneous logins.** Detected simultaneous logins can terminate the whole team's attempt.

## Submissions

- **Max 5 submissions/day** across the 3 days — the submit button disables after that. Don't burn these on untested formatting.
- Keep version history of every submission — shortlisting is based on submitted solutions, and final source code may be requested later.
- Two leaderboards (Private + Public); shortlisting considers both.

## Artefacts required for the best submission

1. A 1–2 page document: ML approach, models used, experiments, conclusion.
2. Source code for experiments, training, and inference — with comments describing functions.

## If shortlisted to the Top 100

Must additionally submit:
- Methodology used
- Candidate generation / blocking strategy
- Model architecture and feature engineering
- Any other relevant approach detail

## Integrity

- No cheating, plagiarism, or unfair practices. Registering/attempting via multiple IDs → instant disqualification.

## Support

- Queries during the hackathon: the organizers' Google Form (linked in the contest email — not reproduced here since it's participant-specific).
- Technical problems: clear browser cache, try a different browser/incognito, try a different network (mobile hotspot vs wifi). If still stuck, email `support@unstop.com` with a screenshot and your registered email — they won't make judgment calls on your behalf, only fix access issues.
